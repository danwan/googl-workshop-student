# Module 4: Compare, evaluate, and transfer

To close the workshop, you compare Notebook and Chat agent on the same decision contract and relate their work pattern to the Workflow used in the primary edition. In the fallback edition, you use the agent-evaluation suite instead of Workflow. You then transfer the method to an enterprise case of your own.

## Learning goals

By the end of this module, you can:

- select tools based on the task rather than personal preference;
- evaluate two answers blindly against the same contract;
- check facts, source quality, and decision logic separately;
- define a small, safe agent use case;
- explain a failed test as evidence of learning, or record that no failure was observed without manufacturing one.

## Choose your path

| Lab | Level | Time | Focus | Observable result |
|---|---|---:|---|---|

| [4.2 Blind evaluation](./lab-4.2-blind-evaluation-scorecard.md) | Practice | 15-20 min | Evaluate content rather than tone | Completed YES/NO matrix |
| [4.3 Transfer to your case](./lab-4.3-transfer-blueprint.md) | Challenge | 20-30 min | Sketch your own safe solution | Six-part implementation blueprint with negative test |

## What good work looks like

The tool choice fits the task, every decision can be verified against sources, and the custom use case has a clear boundary and a test for missing information.
